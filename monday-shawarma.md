# Sheet-Pan Chicken Shawarma with Pita + Cucumber-Yogurt Salad

Makes dinner for 2 adults + 2 small kids, plus 2 adult lunches.

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1.1 kg / 2.4 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 30 g olive oil
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
- 1½ tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- Juice of ½ lemon

### Cucumber-yogurt salad

- 300 g Greek yogurt
- 1 large cucumber, diced
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated, optional
- Dill or parsley, optional

### To serve

- 6–8 pitas
- Chopped tomato, optional
- Hummus, optional
- Feta, optional

## Method

1. Heat oven to **450°F / 230°C**.
2. Toss the chicken and onion with olive oil, spices, salt, pepper, and lemon juice. Spread across a large sheet pan. The chicken can overlap slightly, but don't pile it up.
3. Roast for **18–25 minutes**, until the thighs are browned at the edges and the thickest pieces are around **175°F**. Thighs are better taken beyond the bare-safe 165°F.
4. While it cooks, combine the yogurt, cucumber, lemon, olive oil, salt, and optional garlic/herbs.
5. Warm the pita for the last few minutes of cooking.
6. Slice the chicken and serve everything separately so the kids can assemble—or not assemble—their own plates.

## Tuesday lunch

Before dinner disappears, set aside roughly **350–400 g cooked chicken/onion mixture**. Divide between two containers with pita or rice, cucumber-yogurt sauce, and whatever vegetables you have.
