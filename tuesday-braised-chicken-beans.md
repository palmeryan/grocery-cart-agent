# Oven-Braised Chicken Thighs with White Beans, Tomatoes + Greens

This is intentionally a large batch: Tuesday dinner plus essentially the same amount for Wednesday dinner. Makes about 8 adult-ish servings; comfortably feeds your family twice.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 kg / 3.3 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tsp kosher salt, divided
- Black pepper
- 15 g olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced or minced
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 2 × 800 g / 28 oz cans whole or crushed tomatoes
- 3 × 425 g / 15 oz cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 350 g chicken stock
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ¼ tsp red-pepper flakes, optional
- 150–200 g baby spinach **or** chopped kale
- 15 g lemon juice
- Parmesan for serving, optional
- Crusty bread for serving, optional

## Method

1. Heat oven to **350°F / 175°C**.
2. Season the chicken with about **1½ tsp kosher salt** and black pepper.
3. In a large Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken in 2 batches for about **2–3 minutes per side**. You're only developing color, not cooking it through. Transfer to a plate. For an even more hands-off version, skip the browning entirely.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add the onion and cook **5 minutes**. Add garlic and cook another minute.
5. Stir in tomato paste and cook for **1–2 minutes**.
6. Add tomatoes, beans, chicken stock, oregano, thyme, optional pepper flakes, and remaining **½ tsp salt**. If using whole tomatoes, break them up with a spoon.
7. Nestle the chicken and any accumulated juices into the beans. The chicken doesn't need to be completely submerged.
8. Cover and transfer to the oven for **35 minutes**.
9. Uncover and cook another **15–20 minutes**, until the sauce has thickened somewhat and the chicken is very tender.
10. Stir in the spinach. If using kale, add it about 10 minutes before the braise finishes instead. Finish with the lemon juice and taste for salt.

Serve in bowls with Parmesan and bread if you want some starch.

## Wednesday

Reheat the whole thing gently on the stove or individual portions in the microwave. If desired, shred the remaining chicken into the beans and serve it over toast, polenta, or pasta.
